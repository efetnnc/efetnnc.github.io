Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Vaktinde app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Efe Tunc (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

Your settings (selected city, notification preferences) are stored only on your own device using local on-device storage. This information is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any server the Service Provider controls.

To calculate prayer times, the city you select is sent to a third-party prayer time calculation API (Aladhan). No precise GPS location is requested or collected by the Application — you choose your city from a fixed list, and the device's actual location is never accessed.

The Application displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To provide and personalize these ads, AdMob and its partners may collect information such as your advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate (IP-based) location. On iOS, the Application asks for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before this data can be used for personalized advertising across other companies' apps; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

The Application sends local notifications (for İftar, Sahur/İmsak, Teravih, and Kadir Gecesi reminders) based on your preferences in Settings. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and do not involve any server.

The Application does not require an account or login, and does not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information directly.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Third Party Access

The Application uses the following third-party services:

  • Aladhan Prayer Times API — used to calculate prayer times for your selected city
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob

The Service Provider does not sell or share your personal information with any third party. The Service Provider may disclose information as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or when necessary to protect their rights or the safety of others.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it, which also permanently deletes your locally stored settings. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Data Retention Policy

Your settings remain on your device for as long as the Application is installed. Uninstalling the Application permanently deletes this data; the Service Provider cannot recover it, as it was never stored on a server.
